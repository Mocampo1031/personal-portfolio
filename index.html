PROJECT #1

“Small Floormats” is a project based on the last text message I sent after accidentally buying floor mats for my car that were way smaller than I expected. What started as a random complaint slowly turns into me overthinking how weirdly tiny they actually look inside the car. The project goes beyond the original text message and turns a super normal situation into something funny, exaggerated, and kind of absurd, focusing on how we sometimes fixate on small everyday things for no reason.

PROJECT #3 My twine game


“The Stairs Remember” is a non-linear game that takes you through different perspectives and situations that can happen while being on stairs during different moments and times. Instead of having one straight story, the game lets you move through random experiences and thoughts, making the whole thing feel more immersive, confusing, and unexpected.
